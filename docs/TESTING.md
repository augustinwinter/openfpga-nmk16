# Testing and project status

## Project status

This repository is primarily a preservation and publication record for the finished Analogue Pocket core. Broader community testing is welcome, and reproducible findings may be documented. Specific issues will be opened up to those specific testers who are willing to address technical problems they find (I am not a professional coder).

If you test the core, feel free to open an issue with clear reproduction details. Issues may be used as a public record of findings.

Future work on related NMK16 titles may happen separately.

## If you report a finding

Useful reports include:

- Analogue Pocket firmware version
- handheld or Dock use
- display mode or relevant video settings
- exact steps needed to reproduce the issue
- whether the issue happens consistently or intermittently
- photos or video when they help demonstrate the behavior
- confirmation that the expected `macross.rom` was used

Please keep reports focused on reproducible behavior. General feature requests or requests for ongoing support may be closed without action
