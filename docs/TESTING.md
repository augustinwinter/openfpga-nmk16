# Testing and project status

## Project status

**Macross Ver.26 is considered complete and frozen.**

This repository is primarily a preservation and publication record for the finished Analogue Pocket core. Broader community testing is welcome, and reproducible findings may be documented, but this is **not an actively maintained support project** and there is no commitment to ongoing feature work, bug fixes, compatibility updates, or continued development of Macross Ver.26.

If you test the core, feel free to open an issue with clear reproduction details. Issues may be used as a public record of findings even when no fix is planned.

Future work on related NMK16 titles may happen separately and should not be interpreted as continued development of Macross Ver.26.

## If you report a finding

Useful reports include:

- Analogue Pocket firmware version
- handheld or Dock use
- display mode or relevant video settings
- exact steps needed to reproduce the issue
- whether the issue happens consistently or intermittently
- photos or video when they help demonstrate the behavior
- confirmation that the expected `macross.rom` was used

Please keep reports focused on reproducible behavior. General feature requests or requests for ongoing support may be closed without action.

## What testing does and does not mean

Community testing is welcome because additional observations may improve the preservation record and identify hardware or configuration differences that were not part of the original validation.

Testing does **not** create a maintenance obligation.

A reported issue may be:

- documented for the historical record
- confirmed but intentionally left unchanged
- closed as outside the scope of the frozen Ver.26 release
- useful to future NMK16 work without resulting in a Macross Ver.26 update

Macross Ver.26 was frozen after its Stage26 validation and physical hardware review. The release should therefore be treated as a completed preservation artifact first, and as an actively evolving software project second.
